# Code of Conduct

> **Language / 语言**: English ｜ [中文](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Our pledge

To build an open and welcoming community, we as contributors and maintainers pledge that
everyone taking part in this project will be free from harassment, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual orientation.

We pledge to interact in a **constructive, professional and respectful** way, and to make
this project good together.

---

## Expected behaviour

- **Keep technical criticism about the work**: critique the code, not the person.
- **Respect disagreement**: it is normal for technical approaches to diverge — explain your
  reasoning and the trade-offs instead of dismissing others.
- **Accept constructive feedback**: review comments exist to make the code better, not to
  judge anyone's ability.
- **Act in the community's best interest**: prioritise the project's long-term health over
  personal preference.
- **Be considerate**: this project is maintained by volunteers in their spare time, so
  replies may not be immediate.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information (such as a home address or email address) without
  explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

---

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests
and discussions) and to behaviour when an individual represents the project in public.

---

## Enforcement

If you experience abuse, harassment or other unacceptable behaviour, please report it to
the maintainers through GitHub:

- Open an [issue](https://github.com/YangObscurity/Interactive-Lecture-Engine/issues) describing
  what happened, or mention **@YangObscurity** in any issue or pull request.
- If you would rather not discuss it publicly, simply say so in the issue — the
  maintainer will then provide a private channel.

All complaints will be reviewed and handled **promptly and fairly**.
Maintainers are obliged to keep the reporter's identity confidential.

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues
that do not comply with this code, and to temporarily or permanently ban any contributor
when necessary.

### Enforcement ladder

1. **Reminder**: explain, privately or publicly, why the behaviour was inappropriate.
2. **Warning**: a formal warning, stating the consequences of a repeat.
3. **Temporary ban**: a period during which participation in project interactions is barred.
4. **Permanent ban**: permanent exclusion from the project.
